- Android: text input. When a field takes text, `RuneView` offers the input method an `InputConnection` (text class, multi-line, sentence capitals and autocorrect, no fullscreen editor) and shows the keyboard, and hides it when the field lets go. Committed text, the composition (`setComposingText`; `finishComposingText` commits it as it stands without resetting the input method), deletions, keys and the editor action reach the focused field, and a hardware keyboard's keys arrive as Rune keys, Ctrl shortcuts included.
