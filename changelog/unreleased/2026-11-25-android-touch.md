- Android: touch. `RuneView` sends every finger of each `MotionEvent`, with the samples batched since the last frame, to Rune's touch handling (tap, scroll and fling, long press as a secondary click, pinch), and `Cx::touch_controls` and `Cx::hover_none` are true there. A host can now give the system's long-press delay with `Runtime::set_touch_long_press` (Android passes `ViewConfiguration.getLongPressTimeout()`, which the accessibility Touch & hold delay raises); Rune's 0.5 s stays the default. `examples/kit-gallery` is now a library with a thin binary, so `examples/android-gallery` runs the gallery on Android (`scripts/android-run.sh --app gallery`), and the APK project moved to `examples/android-host`.
