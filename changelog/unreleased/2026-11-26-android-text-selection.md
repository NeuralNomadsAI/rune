- Android: text selection as the system's. A long press on selectable text or in a field selects the word under it; `RuneView` hangs the system's handles on the selection's ends, shows the text's menu (Cut, Copy, Paste, Select all) as the floating toolbar and, while a handle is dragged, the magnifier. A tap in a field brings the caret's handle, which drags the caret and shows the toolbar when tapped. A scroll under selected text keeps it: a finger changes the selection when it lifts as a tap.
