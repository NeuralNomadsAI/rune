- Android: `Tx::pick_file`, `Tx::pick_files` and `Tx::pick_save_file` show the system's document picker (`ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT`, `ACTION_CREATE_DOCUMENT`). Each picked `content://` document is a `FileHandle` from a provider that opens it through the `ContentResolver`'s detached descriptor, with nothing copied; the dialogs that answer with a path and `Tx::open_folder` are refused there with an error.
