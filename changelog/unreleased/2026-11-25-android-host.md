- Android: a first host. `RuneActivity` shows a Kotlin `RuneView` (a `SurfaceView` paced by `Choreographer`, in `crates/rune/android/`) that Rune draws into through `LayerHost`, which now also takes an `ANativeWindow` and can drop and retake its surface while the app keeps its state. An app exports the function calling `run` with `rune::android_main!`; on Android `run` hands the app to the view and returns. The host follows the activity's lifecycle (frames only while resumed with a surface), density, system bars and display cutout as the safe area, the keyboard as a raised bottom inset rather than a resize, dark mode and the back gesture. stdout and stderr go to logcat. Adapters with 16 KB uniform bindings (Android GLES drivers) now get wgpu's downlevel limits. `scripts/android-run.sh` builds `examples/android-hello` and runs it; `ANDROID.md` describes it. Touch, text input and accessibility are not wired yet.
