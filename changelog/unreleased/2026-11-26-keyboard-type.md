- `El::keyboard_type(KeyboardType)` (and rune-kit's `TextInput::keyboard_type`) says what a text field holds, for a touch keyboard to lay itself out by: `Text` (the default), `Email`, `Url`, `Number`, `Decimal` or `Phone`. Android's keyboard shows the keys for it, and a `secure` number gets a PIN's; other hosts ignore it. It refuses no text: a pasted letter still goes into a number field.
