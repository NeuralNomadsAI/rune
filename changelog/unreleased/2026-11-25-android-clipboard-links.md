- Android: the clipboard and links. Copy, cut and paste go through `ClipboardManager` (text, and one image as PNG through `RuneClipboardProvider`, which the app's manifest declares; a secret copy is marked sensitive), `clipboard::revision` counts the system's clipboard changes, and `Tx::open_url` / `Tx::open_url_then` start `Intent.ACTION_VIEW` and report `OpenUrlError::Failed` when no app takes the link. HTML, RTF and files aren't copied or pasted there.
