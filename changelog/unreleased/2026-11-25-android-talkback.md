- Android: accessibility. With the `accessibility` feature, `RuneView` gives TalkBack and other services an `AccessibilityNodeProvider` over Rune's AccessKit tree, through `accesskit_android`'s adapter: roles, names, states, bounds, focus, explore by touch and actions (activate, focus, scroll, text). The view itself is a passive root and, from Android 9, each text is a stop of its own. The tree is built only once a service asks. While a viewport scrolls its changes go at most every 100 ms, and at once when the scroll rests, so a fling doesn't diff the tree and raise events on every frame.
