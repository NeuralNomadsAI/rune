- `Tx::pick_file`, `Tx::pick_files` and `Tx::pick_save_file` are `Tx::open_file`, `Tx::open_files` and `Tx::save_file` answered with a `FileHandle` where those answer with a path: its `name()`, its `path()` where the platform has one, `read()` and `write()` futures for the whole file, and `reader()` and `writer()` to stream it (all for a worker thread). On Android they show the system's document picker and the handle opens its `content://` document through the `ContentResolver`, with nothing copied; the path dialogs and `Tx::open_folder` are refused there with an error. `Runtime::file_dialog_handles` says whether the pending dialog takes handles, and an embedding host that shows file dialogs itself keeps them from being refused (`host.file_dialogs`).
